Fighter Plane

Blue vs. red vs. orange, 1,500 troops a side — a real three-way war, red and orange fight each other too, not just blue. Blue troops also take potshots at any of your aerial bot enemies flying overhead. Everyone starts at peace — nobody fights until you hit the ⚔️ Order the Attack button, then it's on for good. Win by wiping out every red and orange trooper before they wipe out the blues. You've got 5 lives; die with lives left and you drop into spectating the battle until you're back on your feet.

Same web-swing physics as Web Swing — real pendulum rope, wall climbing, rooftop grapples — but you keep your gun, and the whole map (towns, cities, forest, bots) is your playground instead of a plane cockpit.

  • All three sides start at peace, wandering near their own home — nobody shoots until you press the ⚔️ Order the Attack HUD button (or the touch equivalent). Once given, it can't be undone: blue troops go on the offensive, and red and orange fight back against blue and each other.
  • You start on foot near blue's home base — planes and tanks are parked out in the world, not something you spawn into. Walk up to one and press E to get in.
  • WASD walks, left click shoots, right click (hold) aims down sights — watch for houses, barriers, and ditches, and help the blue troops hunt down the reds and oranges — troops and planes never target you, only a crash can kill you
  • E — get in a nearby plane or tank while on foot; press E again to get back out
  • In a plane — Mouse look steers the nose (always flies forward), left click (hold) fires the machine gun, Q fires a guided missile (steer it with WASD mid-flight), B bails out with a parachute
  • Diving builds speed, climbing bleeds it off — climb too hard for too long and you'll stall (nose drops until you dive to recover)
  • In a tank — WASD drives, Mouse aims the turret, left click fires an explosive shell
  • N — nuke: a huge blast that instantly destroys every bot on the map
  • G — throw a grenade, a real gravity arc that explodes on impact or after a fuse
  • F — call your plane back (works on foot or mid-swing) — climbs you back into flying on the spot
  • You have 5 lives — nothing targets you, but flying into the ground or another plane is still a fatal crash; dying drops you into spectating a random blue trooper until you respawn on foot, and only your 5th death ends the round for good.
  • 🕸️ Spider-Man role: E shoots/releases a web (aim near a rooftop to grapple straight up instead of swinging), W/S reel the rope in/out, A/D pump for speed while swinging, Space jumps or pushes off a wall while climbing — run at a building to grab and climb its wall.
