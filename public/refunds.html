Refund policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

  • Cancel anytime. Use Manage subscription on the Premium page. You won't be charged again, and your plan keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Changed your mind? If you ask within 7 days of your first payment for a plan, we'll give you a full refund.
  • Bought by mistake, or a child bought it without permission? Contact us and we'll refund it.
  • Renewals after the first payment aren't usually refunded, but contact us if something went wrong and we'll sort it out.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with and usually take 5–10 days to show up.
