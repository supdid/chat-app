Web Swing

Sling webs between skyscrapers and swing through the city like a superhero. Collect glowing orbs as you go.

  • WASD — run (reel in/out & pump the swing while airborne, or climb/shuffle while on a wall)
  • Mouse — click the city to look around / aim
  • E (or Left Click) — shoot a web at the crosshair / let go. Aim near a rooftop to grapple straight up onto it; aim lower to swing from it
  • F (or Right Click) — web strike. Aim at another player up close to hit them — three hits eliminates them (they respawn at the tower; you get bonus points)
  • Scroll wheel — zoom the camera in and out
  • Space — jump (or push off a wall while climbing). Hold it while swinging to climb up the web toward the anchor
  • Move into a building's wall to grab on and crawl it — works while running straight at it or strafing past it
  • Shift (in the air) — dive. Tuck and drive downward to trade height for speed, steered by where you aim
  • Pump A/D while swinging to build momentum — let go at the right moment for a huge boosted jump
  • Orbs grabbed without touching the ground stack an air chain, worth up to +5 each — landing resets it
  • Watch the charge bar while swinging. It turns amber when you're charged and pulses green the instant you're charged and rising — let go then for a perfect release, worth bonus points and a bigger launch
  • Near misses — skim a building at speed to score. String them together for a rising multiplier
  • Don't hit the ground hard. Slamming in at speed wipes you out: you lose the air chain and control for a moment
  • Joystick (bottom-left) — run, reel/pump while swinging, or climb/shuffle while on a wall
