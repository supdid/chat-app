Web Swing

Sling webs between skyscrapers and swing through the city like a superhero. Collect glowing orbs as you go.

  • WASD — run (reel in/out & pump the swing while airborne, or climb/shuffle while on a wall or ceiling)
  • Mouse — click the city to look around / aim
  • E (or Left Click) — shoot a web at the crosshair / let go outside; punches a nearby bad guy instead once you're indoors. Aim near a rooftop to grapple straight up onto it; aim lower to swing from it
  • Scroll wheel — zoom the camera in and out
  • Space — jump (or push off a wall while climbing). Press it again in mid-air near a ceiling to grab on and crawl across it
  • F — also punches a bad guy standing close to you
  • Move into a building's wall to grab on and crawl it — works while running straight at it or strafing past it
  • Walk up to a building's 🚪 door to go inside — every building has rooms, and some have a bad guy waiting: you have 10 hearts (lose 2 per hit), he has 5 (loses 1 per punch)
  • Pump A/D while swinging to build momentum — let go at the right moment for a huge boosted jump
  • Joystick (bottom-left) — run, reel/pump while swinging, or climb/shuffle while on a wall or ceiling
  • Drag the screen — look around / aim
  • Pinch with two fingers — zoom the camera in and out
  • 🕸️ button — shoot a web at the crosshair / let go outside; punches a nearby bad guy instead once you're indoors. Aim near a rooftop to grapple up onto it; aim lower to swing from it
  • ⤒ button — jump (or push off a wall while climbing). Tap it again in mid-air near a ceiling to grab on and crawl across it
  • 👊 button — also punches a bad guy standing close to you
  • Move into a building's wall to grab on and crawl it — works while running straight at it or strafing past it
  • Walk up to a building's 🚪 door to go inside — every building has rooms, and some have a bad guy waiting: you have 10 hearts (lose 2 per hit), he has 5 (loses 1 per punch)
  • Pump the joystick side to side while swinging to build momentum — let go at the right moment for a huge boosted jump
